## 💫 About Me
- I build systems that turn messy real-world documents — scans, forms, legal text, handwriting — into structured, queryable data.
- I fine-tune and evaluate OCR and vision-language models end to end: data synthesis, LoRA / full SFT, quantization, and serving.
- My main focus is Persian / Farsi document understanding — collecting, cleaning, labeling, and structuring legal and official text at scale.
- I work across the whole pipeline: text detection, word segmentation, orientation and layout analysis, recognition, and post-OCR classification.

## 📌 Focus Areas
`OCR` `Document AI` `Vision-Language Models` `Persian NLP` `Layout Analysis` `Synthetic Data` `Model Fine-tuning` `Model Serving`

**Model stacks I work with:** Surya, PaddleOCR / PaddleOCR-VL, LightOnOCR, Qwen-VL, DeepSeek-OCR, YOLO (detection &amp; segmentation), SAM.

## 🔭 Currently Working On
- Training and evaluating OCR2 / VLM recognizers on Persian legal and official corpora.
- Synthetic text-image generation to cover rare fonts, layouts, and degradations.
- LoRA / bf16 SFT pipelines with Unsloth and TRL, plus vLLM-based evaluation and serving.
- Internal labeling and review tools for detection, recognition, and document classification.

> Most of my work lives in private repos while it's in progress — reach out if you'd like to know more about any of it.
